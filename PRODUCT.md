# SableStone Brokerage

## Product purpose

SableStone is a software-controlled commission broker for recycled, prime and
non-prime polymers. It discovers and qualifies suppliers and buyers,
standardizes offers and demands, matches compatible requirements, negotiates
inside deterministic policy, protects the introduced relationship before
identity disclosure and embeds SableStone's commission into an approved
independent settlement arrangement.

## Commercial boundary

The registered supplier remains seller of record. It owns the resin, sets its
net price, issues the material invoice, owns the quality obligation and ships
to the buyer. The buyer purchases from and pays the supplier through an
approved settlement arrangement. SableStone supplies brokerage services and
receives only its separately stated brokerage entitlement.

SableStone never takes title, possesses inventory, buys or resells resin,
holds buyer purchase money, finances cargo, grants credit, guarantees payment
or quality, transports goods, performs inspection, imports, exports or acts as
customs broker. In particular, it never acts as customs broker for either
counterparty.

## Release promise

The finished release automates discovery, communications, qualification,
supply and demand books, deterministic matching and pricing, bounded
negotiation, KYB/risk, protected agreements, settlement routing, shipment and
document monitoring, double-entry reconciliation, recurring rematching and
acquisition learning.

`BUILD_VERIFIED` proves this chain only with offline fixtures and fake
providers. It does not prove legal classification, tax treatment, provider
approval, market demand, a funded trade, revenue or profitability.

## Constitutional invariants

- `SABLESTONE_INVENTORY = 0`
- `SABLESTONE_CARGO_CAPITAL = 0`
- `SABLESTONE_CREDIT_EXPOSURE = 0`
- identities remain sealed until both parties accept current terms and the
  fee is independently locked;
- unavailable, ambiguous, expired or under-review capability rejects the
  affected transition;
- requests outside policy are declined or expire; there is no founder queue;
- live outreach, accounts, deployment, payment and trading remain disabled
  until their explicit Plan 66 operator gates.

## Evidence truth

Official source receipts, professional advice, agreement versions, provider
approvals, documents, counterparty facts and settlement events retain source,
time and digest. Missing or expired evidence is `UNKNOWN`. No synthetic buyer,
supplier, stock, demand, approval, savings, commission or outcome appears as a
real claim.
